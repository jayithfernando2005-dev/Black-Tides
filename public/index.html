    The Sonar Reload

    1. Every shot uses one cannonball. When you run low, press R.
    2. The sonar shows a picture from the Fish API. Count the fish to reload.
    3. The battle does not stop while you count.
    4. A wrong count jams the cannons for a few seconds.
    5. Counting the chests is a gamble: right pays 25 gold, wrong costs 10 hull.

    Waves

    • Each wave brings more raiders. After a wave you repair 20 hull and visit the Shipyard.
    • Every 5th wave the raider flagship attacks.
    • From wave 6, storms bring rain and a wind that pushes your ship.
    • Sail over crates for a Shield, Rapid fire or Repair.
